AUTONOMOUS ALL-TERRAIN ROVER

Engineered by the Student Innovation Council at TGPCET Idea Lab. Built on a heavy welded structural steel frame with independent double-wishbone coilover suspension, high-torque planetary geared drive motors, dual linear steering actuators, and edge AI navigation.

INTERACTIVE 360° VIEW • HARDWARE CALLOUTS

PROPULSION

High-Torque Hub Motors

Geared planetary motors paired with agricultural deep-lug chevron tires delivering high static stall torque across uneven terrain.

2
KINEMATICS

Linear Actuator & Coilover Suspension

Long-travel red coilover dampers on articulated double-wishbone arms paired with linear actuators for active suspension and steering.

3
POWER ELECTRONICS

MOSFET Motor Drivers & Relay Bank

Dual high-amperage H-bridge motor driver circuits with optical isolation and thermal management for heavy motor surges.

4
ENERGY STORAGE

Dual Lead-Acid Power Supply

Heavy-duty dual battery bank providing clean, high-discharge current to the traction motors while shielding microcontroller logic.

5
AUTONOMOUS NAVIGATION

Raspberry Pi & 4K Webcam Rig

Raspberry Pi onboard SBC running OpenCV vision pipelines fed by an ultra-HD 4K camera mount for spatial mapping and path tracking.

Engineered Hardware Architecture

Designed for harsh industrial and field navigation, every component is selected for high load capacity, electrical isolation, and edge autonomous perception.

⚡

High-Torque Drive Motors

Geared planetary DC motors delivering exceptional stall torque for climbing steep inclines and navigating muddy or gravel terrain with zero wheel slip.

CONFIGURATION Independent 4-Wheel Drive
🎛️

MOSFET Motor Drivers

Heavy-current H-bridge driver bridges equipped with PWM speed modulation, regenerative braking protection, and isolated microcontroller logic feeds.

CURRENT RATING High-Amperage Dual Channel
🦾

Linear Actuators & Suspension

Heavy-duty motorized linear actuators for active suspension leveling, steering articulation, and payload tilt mechanisms on tough terrain.

SUSPENSION Coilover Wishbone
🧠

Raspberry Pi + 4K Vision

Powered by a Raspberry Pi running Linux and OpenCV. An integrated 4K wide-angle webcam delivers real-time spatial vision, lane detection, and obstacle classification.

PERCEPTION 4K Edge Vision (OpenCV)
🔋

Dual Lead-Acid Power Supply

High-capacity rechargeable lead-acid power pack providing high surge amps for the heavy drive motors, backed by step-down buck regulators for clean 5V compute.

POWER RAILS Dual SLA + Isolated Buck
🛠️

Welded Tubular Steel Chassis

Precision fabricated at TGPCET Idea Lab using square-section tubular steel MIG welding, reinforced strut brackets, and heavy agricultural tires.
