HUMANOID BIOMIMETIC HEAD

EZ-INMOOV

Life-Size 3D-Printed Humanoid Cranium with ESP32 & Vision AI

Engineered by the Student Innovation Council at TGPCET from Gael Langevin's open-source InMoov architecture. Built with precision 3D-printed modular plates, high-torque servos, and dual-camera spatial computer vision.

As you scroll, the outer 3D-printed shell pulls apart: the upper cranium dome lifts, the face mask floats forward, and the jaw drops away, uncovering the hidden mechatronic framework.

ELECTRONICS & ACTUATION

ESP32 Core & Servos Revealed

Underneath the separated face plate sits the active hardware core:

  • ◈ ESP32-WROOM-32 — Dual-core processor handling servo kinematics, WiFi telemetry, and I2C sensor bus.
  • ◉ MG996R Metal Servos — 15 kg·cm high-torque servos driving mandible speech actuation and cervical neck yaw.
  • ◆ Color-Coded Wire Loom — Dedicated servo signal cables, DuPont jumper traces, and 6V high-current power buses.
CERVICAL ARCHITECTURE

Multi-Axis Neck Motion

The neck mechanism replicates human cervical kinematics: 180° pan (look left/right), 60° tilt (nod up/down), and 40° roll driven by dual high-torque servos and steel tie rods.

COMPREHENSIVE TEARDOWN

Technical Architecture

Engineered modularly across mechanical, electronic, and software layers for seamless research and expansion.

🧠

Core Processing

  • Primary Controller ESP32-WROOM-32 / ESP32-CAM
  • Co-Processor PCA9685 16-Ch PWM I2C
  • Vision Framework OpenCV / MediaPipe AI
  • Speech Recognition Offline Neural Vocoder
  • Audio Output 3W Speaker + PAM8403
⚙️

Kinematics & Servos

  • Neck Pan (Yaw) 1x MG996R (180°)
  • Neck Tilt (Pitch) 1x DS3218 (20 kg·cm)
  • Neck Roll 1x MG996R (±25°)
  • Jaw Articulation 1x MG996R / MG90S
  • Eye Mechanism 3x SG90 (Pan/Tilt/Eyelids)
👁️

Sensors & Vision

  • Vision Capture Dual OV2640 / CMOS Cams
  • Resolution 1080p @ 30 FPS
  • Proximity Detection HC-SR04 Ultrasonic
  • Sound Tracking Dual Electret Mic Array
  • Visual Indicators Addressable WS2812B Rings
🛠️

Fabrication & Power

  • Architecture InMoov Open Source V1.2
  • Print Material PLA+ White / Carbon Matte
  • Print Duration ~140 Print Hours
  • Power Supply 6V 12A DC Regulated Switch
  • Total Weight ~2.8 kg (Head + Neck Base)

InMoov is an open-source project created by Gael Langevin under CC BY-NC 3.0. Enhanced, 3D printed, programmed and maintained by the Student Innovation Council, TGPCET.
