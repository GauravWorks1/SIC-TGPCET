WHAT IS SIC?

The Student Innovation Council is not a club you join for the certificate. It's a student-run engine room where people from every department — CSE, Mech, ENTC, Civil, and beyond — collide ideas until something new exists.

We prototype robots, write code at 2 AM, break things in the Idea Lab, fix them better, and ship projects that go from whiteboard sketch to working hardware. No hierarchy of branches. Just builders.

  • ▸ Electronics & soldering benches
